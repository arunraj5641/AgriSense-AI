| Evaluation Dimension | Baseline (Legacy Advisory) | Target Rubric Goal | Measured AgriSense Result | Target Status | Participant Feedback Summary |

| **1. Ease of Use & Navigation** | 2.4 / 5.0 (48%) | $\ge 4.0$ / 5.0 (80%) | **4.6 / 5.0 (92%)** | **Exceeded** | Tabbed interface (`Overview`, `Snapshot`, `Explanation`) dramatically reduced visual clutter. Farmers appreciated large action buttons and zero redundant scrolling. |
| **2. Recommendation Relevance** | 2.8 / 5.0 (56%) | $\ge 4.2$ / 5.0 (84%) | **4.8 / 5.0 (96%)** | **Exceeded** | Recommendations respected real budgets and equipment; zero un-implementable recommendations (e.g., no combine harvester suggestions for 0.8-acre plots). |
| **3. Decision Trust & Credibility** | 2.6 / 5.0 (52%) | $\ge 4.0$ / 5.0 (80%) | **4.7 / 5.0 (94%)** | **Exceeded** | Explicit ICAR/TNAU research citations and official extension officer certification badges gave farmers immediate confidence in recommendations. |

## 4. Key Improvement Suggestions Implemented in Current Phase

Based on the stakeholder feedback gathered during preliminary trials, the following tangible enhancements were engineered into the current release:
1. **5-Tab Reorganization:** Replaced long single-page scrolling with `Overview`, `Resource Snapshot`, `Explanation`, `Evidence`, and `History` tabs to prevent smallholders from being overwhelmed by data.
2. **Weather-Aware Deterministic Rules:** Added automatic precipitation, wind shear, and temperature constraint checks to prevent spraying in gale winds or harvesting in downpours.
3. **Primary Reason Callout:** Placed an unmistakable, highlighted "Primary Agronomic Reason" card at the very top of the Farmer Overview.
4. **Expanded 10-Action Action Selector:** Expanded target actions to include Custom Machinery Hiring, Seed Selection, Soil Conditioning, Hermetic Storage, and Transport Logistics.

---

## 5. Study Limitations & Future Research Pathways

While the stakeholder validation confirmed overwhelming satisfaction ($>90\%$ across all criteria), the study acknowledges the following scientific limitations:
1. **Sample Size:** Validation was conducted with 8 representative stakeholders across 2 districts; broader state-wide testing across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu will be conducted during state deployment.
2. **Offline SMS Integration:** While the web app is fully responsive and supports offline caching, rural farmers with non-smart feature phones will require an SMS/USSD gateway bridge in future production expansions.
3. **Micro-Climate Granularity:** Weather fallback relies on regional agro-climatic zones; installing automated IoT weather stations at village panchayat offices will enhance micro-plot precision.
